 About the Energy Survey
 

 

 

 

 
 
 
 

 The Energy Survey results reveal changes in several indicators of energy activity, including drilling, capital spending, and employment. Firms also indicate projections for oil and gas prices. All results are diffusion indexes – the percent of firms indicating increases minus the number of firms indicating decreases.
