Powder River Basin Avian Monitoring and Conservation
In partnership with the U.S. Bureau of Land Management and the Wyoming Game &
Fish Department, EOG has led a study since 2014 to collect real-time, year-round data
on Ferruginous Hawks, a raptor species that nests throughout the PRB. Details on
territory and migration patterns allow us to better support the breeding and nesting of
Ferruginous Hawks and manage our operations to avoid impacts. This study, along with
other conservation efforts in the state, earned EOG the Industry Wildlife Stewardship
Award from the Wyoming Game & Fish Department in 2022.
