All firms What do you believe is the absolute minimum inventory level for the U.S. Strategic Petroleum Reserve (SPR), defined as the point at which no more oil can be practically withdrawn? The largest share of executives surveyed—31 percent—believe the absolute minimum inventory level for the U.S. Strategic Petroleum Reserve is more than 100 million barrels (mb) but not more than 150 mb.
