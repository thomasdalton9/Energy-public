Make no mistake, these are economic locations, but we view secondary zones as unable to keep up with global crude oil demand in the next five years. Hence why we are seeing larger independents make forays into Turkey, Argentina and the like. AI has helped reduce our effective well costs, not through a single measurable dollar impact, but through broad productivity gains across our office.
