Break-even prices for U.S. oil producers—major shale plays

 


 

 
 
Since the 
Dallas Fed Energy Survey
&rsquo;s inception in 2016, the first-quarter survey has consistently included questions asking what WTI oil price is needed to profitably drill new wells and cover existing wells' operating expenses. The following charts show the responses from 
first quarter 2026
. | 

Download the data

.



 

 







 -->






 

 







Since the 
Dallas Fed Energy Survey
&rsquo;s inception in 2016, we have consistently asked energy executives what WTI oil price is needed to (a) profitably drill a new well and (b) to cover operating expenses for existing wells.:





In the top two areas in which your firm is active: What WTI oil price does your firm need to profitably drill a new well?


In the top two areas in which your firm is active: What West Texas Intermediate (WTI) oil price does your firm need to cover operating expenses for existing wells?
