Media Careers Contact Us Train 7 NLNG’s Train 7 Project is an important milestone in our journey of growth, innovation, and sustainability. Building on our drive to inspire a sustainable future, Train 7 will increase our production capacity by 35%, from 22 Million Tonnes Per Annum (mtpa) to 30 mtpa, solidifying Nigeria’s position as a top-tier LNG supplier. Since commencing operations in 1999 with a two-train plant, NLNG rapidly expanded to a six-train facility, delivering over 6,000 LNG cargoes to international buyers. This remarkable growth has earned us a reputation for operational excellence, which the Train 7 project will enhance. The Final Investment Decision (FID) for Train 7 was made on December 27, 2019. In May 2020, the Engineering, Procurement, and Construction (EPC) contract was awarded to the SCD JV Consortium, which includes affiliates of Saipem, Chiyoda, and Daewoo. Train 7 will further harness Nigeria’s vast 202 trillion cubic feet of proven gas reserves - the 9th largest globally - to meet growing global energy demands in an energy transition era. The project underscores NLNG’s commitment to innovation, environmental stewardship, and value creation for Nigeria. Train 7 is a commitment to a sustainable energy future. By leveraging advanced technologies and best practices, the project will minimise environmental impact while maximising efficiency and safety. A Project of National Importance The NLNG Train 7 Project is a milestone not only for NLNG but for Nigeria’s economic and industrial development. By prioritising local content in our supply chain and operations, Train 7 will contribute to a stronger, more resilient national economy, creating opportunities for growth, skills development, and wealth creation. Train 7 embodies NLNG’s vision of “improving lives sustainably”. It is not only a critical driver for gas development in Nigeria, but also a source of significant community development and Nigerian Content benefits in the Niger-Delta region of the country. Also, Train 7 is creating thousands of jobs, boosting local content, and strengthening the Nigerians economy. It will position NLNG to play a leading role in the global energy transition, providing cleaner energy solutions and supporting global climate goals. With over 80% achieved in the construction of the train, the project has recorded more than 70 million safe manhours and it is progressing steadily towards completion.
