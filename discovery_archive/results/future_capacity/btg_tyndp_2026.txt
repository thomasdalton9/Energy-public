                                        INTRODUCTION

Bulgartransgaz EAD Ten-year network development plan has been prepared pursuant to Art.
81 (d), para. 1 of the Energy Act (EA). The Plan covers the period 2026-2035 and sets out the
vision for development of the company as an independent transmission operator and storage
facility operator.
Тhe vision for development is consistent with the main European, regional and national
priorities related to ensuring security of natural gas supply, diversification of natural gas supply
sources and routes and establishing a single free and competitive pan-European gas market,
as well as supporting the decarbonisation of economy and implementation of European Union
(EU) policies in the field of climate and environment.
The priority projects and activities of Bulgartransgaz EAD for the period 2026-2035 aim at
development, modernization and expansion of the natural gas transmission and storage
infrastructure. Their implementation will strengthen Bulgaria's strategic role in the region,
contribute to increase of diversification and security of supplies, and create conditions for
expansion of gasification, as well as support of decarbonisation processes of the energy sector
and economy.
In line with the objectives to reduce carbon emissions, Bulgartransgaz EAD plans projects in
the field of hydrogen for construction of completely new infrastructure for transport of pure
hydrogen and retrofitting of the existing gas transmission network for transport of hydrogen-
gas mixtures.
The Ten-Year Plan aims to to ensure maximum transparency and awareness of the future
development prospects and the Company projects. The document analyses the trends and
factors that justify the need for the planned investments, as well as the indicative periods for
their implementation. That provides support to market participants and facilitates making long-
term investment decisions.
The implementation of the investment strategy set in the Plan will create favourable conditions
in support of diversification of the sources and routes for natural gas supply and for expansion
of its use in the country and the region, bringing significant economic, social and environmental
benefits.
The envisaged investments will foster the development of a competitive and efficient gas
market, ensuring greater supply and opportunities for market participants. This in turn will
promote price efficiency and contribute to development of a liquid and sustainable natural gas
market.
In order to ensure transparency and balance between the interests of the TSO and the market
participants, the Ten-year plan is subject to public consultation enabling synchronization of the
interrelationships between Bulgartransgaz EAD projects and the development plans of the
stakeholders.
The national ten-year plans for network development of transmission operators serve as basis
for drawing up of the EU Community-wide network development plans, developed by the
European Network of Transmission System Operators for Gas (ENTSOG).

Gas infrastructure, owned by Bulgartransgaz EAD on the territory of the Republic of Bulgaria
consists of gas transmission network and an underground gas storage facility in Chiren (Chiren
UGS) connected to it.
Gas transmission network infrastructure provides natural gas transport to users in the
country, as well as cross-border natural gas transport through interconnections with the
neighbouring countries Türkiye, Greece, Serbia, Romania and North Macedonia. It comprises
of 3 484 km gas pipelines and gas pipeline branches, compressor stations – Kardam, Valchi
Dol, Polski Senovets, Rasovo, Provadia, Nova Provadia, Lozenets, Strandzha, Ihtiman and
Petrich of 374 МW approximate total installed capacity, electrochemical protection system,
pigging facilities (PF), communication system, information system and other auxiliary facilities.
In Underground Gas Storage Chiren there are 24 exploitation wells and a compressor
station of approximately 31 МW total installed capacity (including the new compressors under
the gas storage facility expansion project). As of May 1, 2025, the storage capacity of Chiren
UGS is 650 mcm (7,004,400 MWh at 10.776 MWh/1000 m3). The withdrawal and injection
capacity, according to the formation pressures and other factors, varies from month to month
within the range from 25,324 MWh/d to 46,876 MWh/d.

1.     NATURAL GAS MARKET IN BULGARIA

1.1. General Market Overview
Bulgartransgaz EAD implements a consistent and targeted policy to improve connectivity with
neighbouring countries by establishing new interconnection points with significant capacity
and maximizing capacity at the existing points.
The existing and planned gas infrastructure provide an opportunity for diversification of natural
gas supplies both to Bulgaria and to the other countries in the region of Southeast Europe.
1.1.1. Natural gas consumption
Natural gas consumption in Bulgaria for 2025 was 27 656 GWh, which shows 1 % decrease
compared to the consumption in 2024 (27 851 GWh).
According to data from the overall energy balance of the National Statistical Institute for 2024,
natural gas had 13.5% share in the primary energy consumption and 12.5% in the final
consumption of fuels and energy.
The share of natural gas in the country's energy balance remains under the average level for
EU countries, but has the potential for sustainable growth, given its significant role as a
transitional fuel to a low-carbon economy, as well as the developing gasification. In the next
years, natural gas consumption is expected to continue to grow.




                                                                                               11
1.1.2. Main natural gas sources
In 2025 the share of natural gas imports in Bulgaria was over 99%. Consumption in the country
is mostly ensured by gas imports from Azerbaijan and liquefied natural gas from terminals in
the region. Local production in the country remains low. In 2025, about 0.1% of natural gas
consumption was provided by local production from Galata field.
Bulgartransgaz EAD works consistently to improve interconnectivity by increasing the technical
transmission capacities at interconnection points and provide opportunities for gas supplies
from various new sources, including LNG.
In implementation of the European objectives to discontinue fossil fuel supplies from the
Russian Federation, Bulgaria and the region increasingly rely on the Southern and the Vertical
Gas Corridor, as well as on LNG terminals in Southeastern Europe for the provision of natural
gas supplies.
In recent years Bulgartransgaz EAD made significant investments in rehabilitation,
modernization and expansion of the Bulgarian gas transmission network, which improved the
interconnectivity level, efficiency, reliability and flexibility of the transmission system.
Significant transmission capacities to and from Bulgartransgaz EAD system have been secured
at the borders with all neighbouring countries. The transmission capacity is unidirectional only
on the border with the Republic of North Macedonia where IP Kyustendil/Zhidilovo is the only
entry point for natural gas supplies.
The implemented projects contribute to diversification of the supply sources, the provision of
a secure access to additional gas quantities from the Central and South-Eastern Europe region
and the establishment of a liquid, integrated and interconnected natural gas market at
European level.
Bulgartransgaz EAD manages its gas transmission infrastructure as a single system with one
balance zone, respectively one Virtual Trading Point (VТТ). The availability of a single balancing
zone and a single VTP provides opportunity to network users and gas traders to work with a
single balancing portfolio.

Balkan Gas Hub EAD
The trading platform of Balkan Gas Hub EAD (BGH) has been operating since the end of 2019.
As a result of the Company's activities, in the last six years gas liquidity in the country has
significantly and and sustainably increased, the practices of exchange trading and increasing
role and share of the spot market (within the day and the day-ahead) have been successfully
introduced. The products in the short-term segment of BGH are standardized short-term
products (STSPs) within the meaning of Regulation 312/2014 establishing a Network Code on
Gas Balancing of Transmission Networks. They have been developed in accordance with the
requirements of Art. 7 of the Regulation and are intended to meet the needs of transmission
system operators for balancing actions, as well as the needs of traders for balancing their
individual portfolio.
The traded natural gas quantities on the short-term segment of BGH in 2025 amounted to
16,453,692 MWh (an increase of 2.08% compared to the traded quantities of 16,118,475 MWh
in 2024). The total number of transactions for 2025 is 52,336 (or 73.47% more than those for
2024, which were 30,170).
An important factor for increased liquidity is the number and activity of members - as of
February 2026, over 125 companies were members of BGH market, including natural gas
traders, production companies, Bulgartransgaz EAD and other gas transmission network
operators. Most of market members are leading Bulgarian companies in the field of natural
gas trade. About 43% of the members on BGH market are foreign companies among which
are leading European traders operating in the EU single gas market. A considerable number
of the foreign customers are from Bulgaria's neighbouring EU member states - Greece and
Romania, as well as from other countries in South-Eastern and Central Europe.
After July 15, 2025, on the anonymous segments of Balkan Gas Hub market operate clearing
and settlement services through a central counterparty - KELER CCP. This leads to complete
elimination of credit risk between trading participants and significantly increases security,
transparency and trust in exchange trading, as the processes are organized in full compliance
with the requirements of EMIR. The introduction of central clearing is a key step towards
harmonizing the activities of BGH with the established European exchange practices and
further supports Bulgarian gas market integration into the EU single market.
Balkan Gas Hub EAD operation is fully in line with the European objectives on establishing an
interconnected and integrated pan-European gas market and is in support of the plans for gas
infrastructure development in Europe. The establishment of a gas trading exchange is actively
supported by the European Commission and is in strict compliance with the needs identified
by the High Level Group for Gas Connectivity of Central and South-east Europe (CESEC).

1.2. Market potential and development prospects
The EU energy policy is based on the principles of energy security, competitiveness and
sustainability. Bulgaria’s strategic geographical location, well-developed gas infrastructure with
a high level of interconnectivity and significant transmission capacity, determine the
development of the market potential in the country. The implemented infrastructure projects
and the ongoing ones strengthen the key role of the country in improving the diversification
of natural gas supply sources and routes, as well as in increasing energy security in the country
and the region.
By Decision No. 15 of the Council of Ministers of 14.01.2026, the participation of BEH EAD with
a 10% share was approved, together with OMV Offshore Bulgaria and NewMed Energy Balkan
Limited, in implementation of the oil and natural gas search and exploration project in the area
"Block 1-21 Khan Asparuh", located in the exclusive economic zone of the Republic of Bulgaria.
In this context, the potential for development of local natural gas production in the Bulgarian
Black Sea water area should also be taken into account.
Despite the relatively low share in final energy consumption, natural gas is important natural
resource with the potential to increase its share in the country's total energy consumption in
the coming years. It plays a significant role as a transitional fuel in the process of
decarbonisation and transition to low-carbon energy and economy. The expansion of natural
gas use in Bulgaria creates conditions for significant reduction in carbon emissions by replacing
more carbon-intensive fuels and by implementing modern high-efficiency technologies based
on natural gas.
In the following years, natural gas consumption in the country is expected to increase, also as
a result of development of new gas-fired power generation plants. In the context of
accelerated energy transition and an increasing share of production from renewable energy
sources (RES), natural gas is establishing itself as a key transitional fuel, providing the
necessary flexibility and reliability of the power system.

   2.6 Current status, market potential and development prospects
According to data of the International Energy Agency (IEA), the global natural gas
consumption in 2025 has increased by about 1% compared to 2024, reaching approximately
4,3 trillion m3. The registered growth is mainly due to increased demand in Europe and North
America. In 2026, global natural gas consumption is expected to increase by about 2% and
reach a new record value, mainly as a result of the increasing gas demand in rapidly developing
Asian markets.
According to Eurostat data, in 2025 gas consumption in the EU has increased by 1.8%
compared to 2024 and has reached 340 bcm.
LNG imports continue to occupy a significant share of the total natural gas imports in the EU.
According to Eurostat data, in 2025 LNG imports equalled to 43% of the total natural gas
imports and reached 146.4 bcm. The main LNG suppliers to European markets are the USA
(55.1 billion m3), Algeria (23.2 billion m3), Russia (12.6 billion m3), Qatar (9.7 billion m3) and
Nigeria (14.6 billion m3).
In 2025, the share of Russian gas imports to the EU amounted to about 12% of the total
imports. Efforts are focused on diversifying the sources and increasing the natural gas supplies
from reliable suppliers, as well as optimizing the existing gas transmission infrastructure and
LNG terminals.
On 02.02.2026, Regulation (EU) 2026/261 came in force on phasing out Russian natural gas
imports and preparing the phase-out of Russian oil imports, improving monitoring of potential
energy dependencies. The Regulation introduces a stepwise ban on imports of Russian natural
gas, aimed at strengthening energy security and increasing the energy independence of the
EU.
The EU is increasing its natural gas imports from various sources such as Norway, the USA,
North Africa, Azerbaijan, Algeria, Qatar, the UK and other alternative suppliers. In 2025, the
imported quantities from Norway and the USA reached up to 147.2 bcm, which represents
approximately 43% of the total gas imports into the EU. The total natural gas quantities
imported from Norway amount to 92.1 bcm and from the USA - to 55.1 bcm.
The concept of enhancing the EU's energy security envisages increasing the supply of LNG to
member states by building infrastructure, ensuring access of domestic markets to the global
LNG market, as well as overcoming infrastructure bottlenecks. The share of LNG in EU imports
is expected to continue to grow in the short and medium term.

The calculation of the N-1 standard was prepared for the period 2026-2030 according to Article
5 of Regulation (EU) No. 2017/1938 concerning measures to safeguard security of gas supply
and repealing Regulation (ЕU) No. 994/2010.
The N–1 formula describes the capability of the technical capacity of the gas infrastructure to
satisfy the total gas demand in the area under calculation in the event of disruption of the
single largest gas infrastructure during a day of exceptionally high gas demand occurring with
a statistical probability of once in 20 years.
In the event of a disruption of the single largest gas infrastructure, the capacity of the
remaining infrastructure should be able to supply the necessary gas quantities to satisfy the
total gas demand in the area under calculation, i.e. N-1>100%.

5.     DESCRIPTION OF KEY PROJECTS

The key infrastructure development projects of Bulgartransgaz EAD described below represent
strategic investments for the country and the region in the context of the decisions at the
European level to phase out the import of Russian natural gas from 2027. The projects for the
implementation of the strategic Vertical Gas Corridor for Europe, the participation in projects



                                                                                                51
related to the regasification of liquefied natural gas, as well as investments in improving the
regional connectivity are key to ensuring the long-term energy security. Therefore, the
company's plans are to implement them in the short term and allow significant quantities of
natural gas from alternative sources to be transported through the gas transmission
infrastructure of Bulgartransgaz EAD both to Bulgaria, and the countries from the region.
The following projects will be key for the energy security, diversification and enabling the
transport of additional natural gas quantities to and through Bulgaria:
   o Expansion of Chiren UGS;
   o The projects for the increase of the transmission capacities from Greece to Bulgaria
     and from Bulgaria to Romania in the context of the strategic Vertical Gas Corridor;
   o Projects for modernisation and expansion of the existing gas transmission network with
     a contribution to ensuring safe and reliable transmission of expected increased
     quantities of natural gas across the country.
Detailed information on the main gas infrastructure development projects is presented in point
5.1.
Along with the efforts that Bulgartransgaz EAD makes to achieve the pan-European goals for
improving the security of supply, diversification of natural gas supply sources and routes, the
Company actively participates in the current processes for adapting the energy sector in
accordance with the plans to achieve of energy decarbonisation and climate neutrality
according to the European energy policy.
The next year will see the natural gas keeping its key role in the process achieving the
decarbonisation objectives. The available gas transmission infrastructure provides an
opportunity for the successful and accelerated introduction of hydrogen into the energy mix,
and the plans for the construction of a new hydrogen transmission infrastructure will guarantee
the large-scale development of the energy sector in the country. This concept is embedded in
defining the projects that will contribute to the transition to low carbon energy. Solutions for
preparation of the existing gas transmission network for compatibility with the inclusion of
hydrogen and other low-carbon gases in the network, as well as for completely new routes for
green hydrogen transmission are being considered that may potentially become the future
hydrogen corridor in the region of South East Europe.
Bulgartransgaz EAD is developing the following hydrogen projects:
   o Hydrogen transmission infrastructure in Bulgaria (in 2 phases);
   o Project for the Smartening of the existing Bulgarian gas transmission system and the
     associated network assets to effectively integrate low carbon and renewable gases.
The implementation of the projects accounts for the development of strategies and policies
regarding the hydrogen infrastructure at regional and pan -European level is taken into
account. Detailed information is available in 5.2 below.
5.1. Key projects for the development of the gas infrastructure
The gas infrastructure development concept is based on several key factors:
   o   New natural gas sources;
   o   Optimal use of the existing gas transmission networks and Chiren UGS;


                                                                                              52
   o   - Modernization and expansion of the existing infrastructure;
   o   Building new, expansion of existing interconnection with neighbouring countries and
       network development to new exit points in the country;
   o   Optimal trade environment through a liquid gas exchange.
The complete ban on the import of Russian natural gas from September 2027 and the need
to increase the import of liquefied natural gas from reliable sources are the factors leading to
a change in the natural gas market in the country and the region.
“In this regard, the consistent work on the implementation of the infrastructure of the Vertical
Gas Corridor will continue as the main highway for transport of liquefied natural gas to the
region. An entry point for the expected additional quantities of liquefied natural gas for the
gas markets in the region from reliable sources, such as the USA, Qatar, Egypt and others, is
Alexandroupolis Independent Natural Gas System (INGS) and Bulgartransgaz EAD has
a 20% shareholding there. The terminal includes an entry point for the Vertical Gas Corridor
initiative as well. The existing and planned gas pipelines in the region will provide access to
natural gas from the terminal for consumers in Bulgaria, as well as in the Republic of North
Macedonia, Serbia, Romania, Hungary, Moldova and Ukraine. The terminal provides them with
the opportunity to take advantage of the dynamically developing LNG market and the benefits
it offers – flexibility, competitiveness and security of supply, as well as access to new gas
suppliers.
All possibilities for Bulgartransgaz EAD's shareholding in a project for a new LNG terminal in
Greece are also being considered to contribute to further diversification of natural gas sources
and strengthen the energy security of both, Bulgaria and the region.
Capacity expansion of Chiren Underground Gas Storage
Chiren UGS is mainly used to compensate the seasonal fluctuations in natural gas consumption
in the country caused by the differences between the supplies and consumption by providing
the necessary flexibility, and ensures emergency reserve.
Chiren UGS capacity expansion project has been determined to be a project of common
interest for the EU. The objective is the capacity increase in stages of the only gas storage
facility on the territory of Bulgaria in order to achieve larger volumes of gas stored - up to 1
bcm, including increased gas reservoir pressure and higher daily average withdrawal and
injection rates up to 8-10 mcm/day.
The expansion of the capacity of the only gas storage in our country is among the priority
energy projects in the region. Its implementation will contribute to guarantee energy security,
the security of supply, and increase competition on the gas market in Bulgaria and South East
Europe. The project is in synergy with the existing and planned new LNG terminals in the
region and the plans for expansion of the gas transmission infrastructure and will enable gas
traders and consumers in the region to take full advantage of the dynamic development and
competitive advantages provided by the liquefied natural gas market.
The project includes three main aspects: (1) design and construction of new surface equipment
– a compressor station with its auxiliary facilities, (2) design and construction of underground
equipment and (3) design and construction of a gas transmission pipeline connecting Chiren




                                                                                              53
UGS to the existing gas transmission system of Bulgartransgaz EAD in the area of village to
ensure the required connectivity.
The transmission gas pipeline was commissioned in November 2024, and the compressor
station and its auxiliary equipment were commissioned in March 2025. This allowed as of 1
May 2025 the capacity of Chiren UGS to be increased by 100 mcm up to a total of 650 mcm.
The storage capacity will be gradually expanded in parallel with construction of the new wells.
The target parameters of the expansion (increasing the volume of active gas to 1 bcm and the
daily withdrawal and injection capacities to 8-10 mcm/day) will be achieved in stages, taking
into account the confirmed interest from the market. The Vertical Gas Corridor Initiative is
a strategic project to ensure the security of supply and diversification of the natural gas supply
for Bulgaria and the countries of Southeastern, Eastern and Central Europe.
The concept of the project is based on the maximum utilization of the possibilities of the
existing gas transmission networks and gas systems along the corridor, including the
Alexandroupolis terminal and making optimal investments of the participating parties in an
additional infrastructure, which will allow the transport of the increasing quantities of LNG from
the LNG terminals in Greece and Türkiye.
The increase of gas transmission capacities at the interconnection points of the gas
transmission networks of Greece, Bulgaria, Romania, Hungary, Slovakia, Ukraine and Moldova
is of key importance for boosting the energy security and market integration of the countries
of South East, Eastern and Central Europe.
The strategic route of the Vertical Gas Corridor Initiative will ensure access to additional
quantities of LNG for consumers. Concerning the projects that will be implemented on the
territory of Bulgaria for the increase of the capacities from Greece to Bulgaria and from Bulgaria
to Romania will ensure the technical possibility for the transport of the additional quantities of
natural gas, including from the Southern Gas Corridor and liquefied natural gas from terminals
in neighbouring countries with the maximum use of the possibilities of the existing
infrastructure.
The projects that Bulgartransgaz EAD works on to increase the transmission capacities
from Greece to Bulgaria and from Bulgaria to Romania are a key part of the overall
vision of the Vertical Corridor, and their implementation and the infrastructure already available
in the country are the basis for achieving the set objectives for diversification and improving
the security of supply.
A project to increase the transmission capacity at Interconnection point
Kulata/Sidirokastro in Greece-Bulgaria direction
The project includes the construction of infrastructure to increase the technical capacity for
gas transmission in IP Kulata/Sidirokastro in the direction from Greece to Bulgaria with 35,4
GWh/d up to a total of 102 GWh/d.
Its implementation will provide access to additional quantities of pipeline gas and LNG from
alternative sources, including the USA, Azerbaijan, Egypt and others for all countries in the
region. As part of the key infrastructure of the Vertical Gas Corridor, the implementation of
the project is a priority for Bulgaria and the region of Central, Eastern and South East Europe.

The significant potential of the Eastern Mediterranean region and South East Europe for both

infrastructure on the territory of Bulgaria was granted the status of a project of pan-European

of mutual interest (PMI) of the EC under Regulation 2022/869 on guidelines for trans-European
energy infrastructure (TEN-E), published on 01.12.2025 (described below as a hydrogen

transmission network in Bulgaria with a transmission possibility from/to Greece, with

stage, which represents a continuation of the hydrogen transmission infrastructure of Phase 1
crossing the interior of the country to the border with Romania.

transmission infrastructure, ensuring cross-border connectivity with Greece and Romania as
well as with the countries of Central Europe.

Europe, which will ensure transmission of clean hydrogen from the region to Central Europe -
both from local production, and imports. The hydrogen transmission infrastructure projects on
the territory of Bulgaria are part of the scenarios for development of the hydrogen networks

construction of a South-East European priority hydrogen corridor from Greece through

upgrade of the existing Greek and Bulgarian gas transmission networks for integration of low-
carbon and renewable gases (SmartSwitch project).

transport natural gas mixtures with up to 10% hydrogen. Activities are planned for the

5.4.1. Overhauls of sections of the gas transmission network
The maintenance activities of the gas pipeline network during its operation found out
amortisation, most of which relates to metal loss. Repairs are carried out in a timely manner
to restore the gas pipelines' strength characteristics and replace their anti-corrosion coating,
according to the prescribed repair programmes.
5.4.2. Overhauls of gas turbine engines and scheduled maintenance and
inspections of GTCUs
All gas turbine engines (GTE) have the so-called between overhaul resource in working
hours (resource until inspection) and general technical resource in working hours which

reliability, safety, efficiency and preservation of the operational parameters of the GTE.
Following expiry of the between overhaul resource or in case of failure of the equipment during
operation, an overhaul shall be carried out to recover the mechanical, environmental and gas
dynamic parameters, as well as to ensure its safe and reliable operation in the future (following
the overhaul) working hours until reaching the general technical resource.

                                        CONCLUSION


Bulgartransgaz EAD is the combined gas operator providing licensed services of natural gas
transmission and storage on the territory of the Republic of Bulgaria. The Company operates
sites of national importance and strategic sites for the energy security of the country, including
Chiren UGS.
In the conditions of a dynamically changing energy environment, the significance of the
Bulgarian gas transmission system as part of the regional energy infrastructure is growing.
Thanks to its geographical location, well-developed infrastructure and high level of
interconnectivity, Bulgartransgaz EAD gas transmission system creates conditions for natural
gas transmission from diversified sources.
The company implements a consistent investment policy aimed at gas infrastructure
development, rehabilitation and expansion in order to increase its reliability, efficiency and
capacity. The implementation of the planned infrastructure projects contributes to
strengthening the energy security of the country and the region, to further diversification of
supply sources and routes, as well as to development and liberalization of gas markets at the
national, regional and European levels.
The main priority infrastructure development activities of Bulgartransgaz EAD for the period
2026-2035 include:
   o Maintenance of technically operational, reliable and high-efficiency main and auxiliary
     gas infrastructure;
   o Development, rehabilitation and expansion of the gas transmission and storage
     infrastructure, including implementation of the projects along the Vertical Gas Corridor
     and Chiren UGS expansion project;
   o Improvement of interconnectivity with neighbouring countries;
   o Expansion of the natural gas storage, injection and withdrawal capacity;
   o Adaptation of the existing gas transmission infrastructure for transportation of natural
     gas mixtures with up to 10% hydrogen;
   o Building new infrastructure for transmission of clean hydrogen.
The implementation of the planned projects and targeted investments to optimise and
maximise the capacity of the existing networks will create conditions for significant increase of
natural gas quantities from alternative sources and reliable partners that will be transported
through Bulgartransgaz EAD gas transmission system, both for the needs of the Bulgarian
market and the countries of Central and Eastern European region.
Bulgartransgaz EAD maintains active cooperation with gas transmission operators in the
region, which contributes to improve connectivity between the national gas systems,
integration of the markets and increase of security of supply.
The Company is among the main initiators of the Vertical Gas Corridor which implementation
will provide the necessary levels of capacity for transmission of the increasing natural gas flows
from south to north, will increase energy security, improve the conditions for diversification of
supplies and contribute to the integration, liberalisation and stability of gas markets in the
region.



                                                                                                70
In the context of the energy transition and the European decarbonisation policies, natural gas
will continue to play an important role as a transition fuel to achieve the EU's 2030 greenhouse
gas reduction targets and a climate-neutral economy by 2050.
Bulgartransgaz EAD develops and plans hydrogen-related projects for integration of renewable
and low-carbon gases into the gas transmission network, as well as construction of new
infrastructure for transmission of clean hydrogen. The company also participates actively in
European initiatives regarding hydrogen economy development.
It will continue to ensure reliable and secure natural gas transmission and storage by
implementing consistent measures to support national and pan-European objectives related to
security of supply, diversification of sources and supply routes, as well as decarbonisation of
the energy system.
