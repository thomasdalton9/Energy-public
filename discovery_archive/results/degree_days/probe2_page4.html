Household Budget Survey - 2025

This release contains data received through the Household Budget Survey, which was conducted in the period from January 1, 2025 to 31 December 2025. The interviewing was done in 1 438 households on the territory of Montenegro.

External trade in goods of Montenegro

The total external trade in goods of Montenegro for January-August 2026 according to preliminary results is 3 366.5 million EUR, indicating a growth of 2.4% in comparison with the same period of the previous year.

Gross domestic product of Montenegro

Real growth rate of gross domestic product for 2025 was 3.3%. This release presents the results of the Gross Domestic Product (GDP) of Montenegro for 2025, compiled according to the production and expenditure approaches, at current and previous year's prices, in accordance with the ESA 2010 methodology.
