Producer prices of agricultural and fishing products in August 2026, compared to the same month of 2025 were decreased by 8.7% average.

12.10.2026. - Consumer price indices by COICOP, September 2026

Prices of goods and services used for personal consumption in September 2026 in relation to August 2026 increased by 0.2% on average.

01.10.2026. - Gross Domestic Product, 2025

Gross domestic product (GDP) in 2025 amounted to RSD 10 558 438 million at current prices. When compared to the previous year, GDP increased by 8,1% in nominal terms, and by 2,1% in real terms.

30.09.2026. - External trade, August 2026

The overall external trade in the Republic of Serbia for January – August 2026 amounted to:

   - USD 61022.8 million - which was an increase of 11.5% compared to the same period last year;

   - EUR 52516.3 million - which was an increase of 6.4% compared to the same period last year.

30.09.2026. - Industrial production indices, August 2026

The industrial production in August 2026 decreased by 0.5% relative to August 2025.

30.09.2026. - Retail trade turnover, August 2026

Retail trade turnover in the Republic of Serbia in August 2026, compared to the same month last year, increased by 8.4% at current prices and by 6.3% at constant prices.

Comparing the retail trade turnover in the first eight months 2026 with the same period 2025, it increased by 8.9% at current prices, and by 7.2% at constant prices.

 

30.09.2026. - Tourist turnover, August 2026

In the Republic of Serbia, 537 thous. tourist arrivals were registered in August 2026, which was by 7.9% more than in the same month of the previous year. A total of 1 582 thous. tourist overnight stays were recorded, representing an increase of 7.3% compared with August of the previous year.

Of the total number of overnight stays, domestic tourists accounted for 851 thous. overnight stays, which was by 5.0% more than in the same period of the previous year, while foreign tourists accounted for 731 thous. overnight stays, representing an increase of 10.1%.

In terms of overnight stays, in August 2026, among urban tourist destinations the most visited were Beograd, Novi Sad and Subotica, among spa resorts Vrnjačka Banja, Sokobanja and Banja Vrdnik, and among mountain centres Zlatibor, Kopaonik and Fruška gora.

In August 2026, the highest number of overnight stays by foreign tourists was realised by tourists from Russian Federation, Turkey and Germany.
