Venture Global Gator Express, LLC

Venture Global Gator Express, LLC was authorized to construct and operate two 42-inch diameter lateral pipelines, which have an aggregate length of approximately 26.8 miles and related facilities. The pipelines are located entirely in Plaquemines Parish, Louisiana, and consist of a 15.1-mile-long lateral and an 11.7-mile-long lateral from planned receipt interconnects with Texas Eastern Transmission, LP, Tennessee Gas Pipeline Company, LLC, and Columbia Gulf Transmission, LLC to the new liquefied natural gas export terminal on the west bank of the Mississippi River constructed by Venture Global Plaquemines LNG, LLC.
